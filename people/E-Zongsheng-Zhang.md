### Introduction

I am a Bioinformatics Research Assistant at the SMART Leyao Wang Lab. Driven by the AI for Science paradigm, I am dedicated to the interdisciplinary integration of computational science, statistics, and biology. My current research focuses on the human microbiome and HIV-1, while also exploring the application of Large Language Models (LLMs) in climate change research.

I possess extensive experience in multi-omics data analysis, encompassing Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS) and Nanopore third-generation sequencing technologies. I am highly proficient in amplicon analysis and RNA sequencing (RNA-seq) workflows.
