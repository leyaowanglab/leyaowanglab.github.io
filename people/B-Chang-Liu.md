### Introduction

I am a Assistant Professor in Leyao Wang Lab,dedicated to exploring the association between microorganisms and the pathogenesis of respiratory diseases. My specific research interests lie in understanding how microorganisms regulate airway epithelial function and modulate the immune microenvironment, as well as their underlying roles and mechanisms in the initiation and progression of respiratory disorders. 

### Research Fields

- Microbiome
- Respiratory Pathogenesis
