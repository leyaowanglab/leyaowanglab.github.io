### Introduction

Junhong Liu is a PhD student from the Westlake-SMART PhD Program in 2025. He has chosen to conduct his second rotation in Leyao Lab. During his master's degree, he has mainly researched on Oligodendrocyte/myelin function in health and diseases. In Leyao Lab , he will perform the microbiome-host immune interaction research through the olfatory sensory neurons isolated from olfactory epithelium.
